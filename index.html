About us

A society built on shared savings, not shareholder profit

FAQ

Common questions

When is interest added to my balance?

At the end of every quarter — 31 March, 30 June, 30 September and 31 December — 4% interest is credited automatically and shown in your account statement.

What is the interest rate on the Kalyan loan?

13% per annum, with a duration you choose based on your loan amount, from 3 months up to 36 months.

Can I sign up with just my phone number?

Yes — use the phone + password option on the sign up page, or continue with your Gmail account.
